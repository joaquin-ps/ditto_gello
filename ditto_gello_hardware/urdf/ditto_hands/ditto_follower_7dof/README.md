# DITTO 7-DoF follower hand

Generated by `scripts/build_ditto_hand.py` as `ditto_hand.urdf` (renamed here to
`ditto_follower_7dof.urdf`); do not edit it or `meshes/` by hand. Exception: joint limits
now follow ditto's `hand_config/7dof_f02` follower limits.

Source: manipulation_env `manipulation_ws/src/ftip_teleop/deprecated/model/7_dof_assembly_urdf`
(commit 8dff3f8, "Updated udrf for most recent 7dof"). It is the SolidWorks export of the hand
(`7_dof_assembly_urdf`, sw_urdf_exporter) with the finger axes and zero rotations edited so
the URDF's joint angles are ditto's follower angles: manipulation_env's fingertip teleop sent
its IK solutions to the follower unchanged. The raw export (same meshes) has other zeros and
signs; the build script refuses it.

- `hand_mount`: rigidly at the xArm flange (`link7`, before the rig's `tcp_offset`).
- `hand_mount_to_palm`: the mount, rpy `0 pi/2 0` from manipulation_env's `ur5_7dof.xacro`
  (the palm's mounting face is its x=0 plane). **VERIFY** the rotation about the flange axis
  at the rig: compare the GUI scene with the real hand.
- Finger joints `index1..3`, `thumb1..4` in ditto's order (follower motors 21 22 23 11 12 13
  14), rad, limits from ditto's `7dof/7dof.yaml`. The xArm service sets them from the DITTO
  service's `q` (`model.hand_service`).
- `ftip_index`, `ftip_thumb`: the fingertip points of the fingertip teleop.
- `meshes/visual`: decimated to ~40k triangles in all (from ~150k; 99% of the surface within
  ~1.5 mm). `meshes/collision`: convex hulls of the full-resolution meshes, the palm in 8
  CoACD parts.

Rebuild (coacd and fast-simplification are only needed for this):

    uv run --with coacd --with fast-simplification python scripts/build_ditto_hand.py \
        ../manipulation_env/manipulation_ws/src/ftip_teleop/deprecated/model/7_dof_assembly_urdf
